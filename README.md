# 👑 DUNGEON OVERLORD
### Your Dungeon. Your Monsters. Your Empire. Your Rules.

**The heroes call you a villain. Your minions call you boss. The goblins are demanding a raise.**

Deep beneath the surface, you rule a sprawling underground dungeon filled with monsters, magic, treasure, and creatures with ambitions of their own.

Your kobolds build, goblins scheme, ratlings bargain, slimes multiply, and an ancient dragon may have opinions about your management style. Meanwhile, adventurers dream of plundering your treasures and earning fame by defeating you.

**What kind of Overlord will you become?**

⚔️ **Conquer or Defend** — Command monster armies, build deadly traps, repel invading heroes, or bring the surface kingdoms to their knees.

🏰 **Build Your Empire** — Expand your dungeon, develop industries, amass treasure, and transform your underground domain into something legendary.

🤝 **Rule Your Way** — Govern through terror, loyalty, diplomacy, cunning, generosity, or sheer administrative brilliance.

🐉 **Manage Your Monsters** — Navigate goblin disputes, kobold inventions, dragon tantrums, undead grievances, and the occasional wraith poetry recital.

🎲 **Expect the Unexpected** — Dynamic dungeon events, bizarre discoveries, political complications, and adventuring parties ranging from hilariously incompetent to genuinely terrifying.

---

### The Dungeon Is Yours. What Happens Next Is Up to You.

Be a ruthless Dark Lord, a benevolent monster king, an eccentric wizard, or an Overlord more interested in running a profitable tavern than conquering the world.

**No chosen destiny. No mandatory quests. No predetermined ending.**

*Just a living dungeon, a world full of possibilities, and a throne with your name on it.*

**Welcome to Dungeon Overlord.**
